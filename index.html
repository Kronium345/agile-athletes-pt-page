What is Agile Athletes?

Agile Athletes is a fitness and wellness app for everyday gym-goers, whether you’re just starting out or chasing a new PB. Learn how to do each exercise properly, train at a level that suits you, and track your steps and progress. When you want company, find training partners, run clubs and personal trainers near you — starting with London and the UK.

Free to start

Download free and start training. Upgrade to Premium when you want more.

  • Free: exercise library with step-by-step guides and anatomy previews
  • Free: Beginner, Standard and Burnout workouts with a rest timer
  • Free: step counter with trophies
  • Free: Fitness Network — training partners, run clubs and gym groups
  • Free: find personal trainers near you
  • Premium: AI Coach and AI Form Coach — film one set, see what to fix
  • Premium: workout history, favourites and Mind Center
